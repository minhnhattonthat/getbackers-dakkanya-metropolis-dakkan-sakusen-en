# One glyph per block: a line "[c]" (the character), then 11 rows: rows 0-8 sit on the baseline (cap height 9,
# x-height 6 = rows 3-8), rows 9-10 are descenders. '#' = ink, '.' = clear; every row has the glyph's width.
# The advance is width + 1 (one clear column between glyphs). Trailing rows may be left out (taken as clear).
